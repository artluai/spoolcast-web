The flow, beat by beat

Each beat shows exactly what's on screen at that moment, starting from blank. You = the user acts. Spoolcast = the drafter responds. Nothing is pre-filled — everything on screen was put there by a previous beat.

BEAT 6

Two more gates of the same shape, then fan-out

Same gesture, repeated: Spoolcast proposes the world & cast, you approve. Per season it proposes the 10-episode list with a brief each, you approve. Approving a season's list triggers fan-out — ten real sessions are created.
